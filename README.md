# **AI Business Analyst Agent**

## **Overview**

An AI-powered Business Intelligence automation workflow built using n8n, Google Gemini, Google Drive, and SMTP Email.

The system automatically processes Excel sales reports, calculates KPIs, generates executive business insights using Generative AI, and emails management reports.

---

## **Features**

* Automated Excel ingestion from Google Drive  
* KPI calculation engine  
* Revenue analysis  
* Product performance analysis  
* Regional analysis  
* AI-generated executive summaries  
* Automated email reporting  
* Business recommendations

---

## **Architecture**

Google Drive  
↓  
Download File  
↓  
Extract XLSX  
↓  
KPI Engine  
↓  
Google Gemini  
↓  
Email Report
